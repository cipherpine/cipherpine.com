// Shipping now

QuotaPane

A small, always‑on‑top desktop window that shows how much of your Claude and Codex subscription quota you have left — read locally, from your own credentials, with no account to create and nothing phoned home.

  • Trust boundary Exactly two modules ever touch a credential or the network — deliberately small enough to read end to end in one sitting.
  • Egress Deny‑by‑default through a single chokepoint, with a compile‑time allowlist of two hosts. Tests prove it on every push.
  • Telemetry None, of any kind, to anyone. No auto‑update and no update check — there is no updater in the codebase at all.
  • Open source Written in Rust. Signed releases with build attestation, verifiable from the artifact. MIT OR Apache‑2.0.
